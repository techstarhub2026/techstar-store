Part SevenStorefront UI/UX Specification

Each screen below is specified as: purpose, layout (region by region, desktop then mobile), elements (every control, its copy, its behaviour), states (loading, empty, error, partial), and interactions (what happens on each action). Copy shown in quotation marks is the exact string to implement; the consolidated copy deck is Appendix C.

7.1.2 Search field

AspectSpecification
Placeholder"Search products, SKUs, part numbers…"
SuggestionsFires after 2 characters, debounced 250 ms. A dropdown of up to 8 results, each a 40 px thumbnail, product name, subcategory eyebrow and price. Below them, "See all n results for 'term'".
Keyboard↓/↑ move through suggestions, Enter opens the highlighted one or submits the raw term if none is highlighted, Esc closes the dropdown and keeps the text.
SubmitNavigates to /search?q=…. The term is logged for analytics once per distinct term per session — not on every keystroke, which is what would make the reference platform's search table useless.
No matchesDropdown shows "No products match 'term'" plus "Browse all products" and "Ask us on WhatsApp".
Accessibilityrole="combobox" with aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-activedescendant; the listbox has role="listbox" and each option role="option". Result count announced in a live region.

7.1.3 Cart and wishlist indicators

  • Cart. Icon plus a pill badge (distinct item count, not total quantity) and the running total formatted per the currency setting — TZS 45,000. Clicking opens the cart off-canvas. Badge animates a single 200 ms scale pulse on change, suppressed under reduced motion.
  • Wishlist. Icon plus count badge. Clicking opens the wishlist off-canvas.
  • Zero state. The badge renders "0" and the total "TZS 0" — matching the reference platform, which always shows the badge. This is preferred to hiding it, because a persistent target is easier to hit and to learn.

7.1.4 Breadcrumb

A home icon linking to /, then >-separated ancestors, the last being plain text in --ts-text-muted. Marked up as <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"> with an ordered list and aria-current="page" on the last item, plus BreadcrumbList JSON-LD. Trails: catalogue → Home > Products; subcategory → Home > {Category} > {Subcategory}; product → Home > {Category} > {Subcategory} > {Product}; content pages → Home > {Page}.

7.1.5 Email verification banner

When a signed-in user's email is unverified, a dismissible bar sits directly beneath the header: "Please verify your email address to receive order updates." with a "Send verification link" button. On send, the button becomes a disabled "Verification link sent" for 60 seconds. Dismissal is remembered for the session only. Never shown to guests.

7.2 Home

Purpose. Give a browsing visitor three different ways into the catalogue — promotion, novelty, popularity — and establish that the store is real, stocked and staffed.

7.2.1 Layout, top to bottom

7.2.2 States

StateRendering
LoadingBanner shows a shimmer block at its final height. Each carousel shows six skeleton cards at final dimensions. No layout shift when real content arrives — this is the single most important performance requirement on the page.
No bannersCarousel region omitted. The page begins with "OUR PRODUCTS". No placeholder, no empty coloured band.
Empty railThat section is omitted. A section heading with nothing under it is never rendered.
Partial failureEach rail loads independently. One failing request degrades one rail to an inline "Could not load this section. Retry" — it never blanks the page.

7.2.3 Product card, definitive specification

This component appears in every grid and carousel in the storefront. Specified once, here.

ElementSpecification
ContainerWhite, 1 px --ts-border, radius md, shadow 1 → 2 on hover. The whole card is a link to the product page; nested action buttons stop propagation.
Image well1:1 aspect ratio, --ts-surface-sunken, object-fit: contain with 8 px inset. Uses the md derivative with an srcset offering sm for narrow viewports. loading="lazy" and explicit width/height to reserve space. alt is the product name.
BadgesDiscount badge top-left, accent amber, "−15%". "NEW" badge top-right, primary green, for products created within 30 days. "OUT OF STOCK" ribbon across the lower third of the image well, red at 90% opacity, when total stock is zero.
EyebrowSubcategory name, 11 px uppercase, muted, centred, truncated to one line.
TitleProduct name, 13 px semibold uppercase, centred, truncated to one line with ellipsis. Full name available as the link's accessible label — never rely on title alone.
PriceCentred, primary green, 15 px semibold. Multi-variant products show "From TZS 200". Discounted products show the original struck through in muted grey before the new price. Zero-price products cannot exist (§12.3), so no zero case is designed.
Action rowTwo 32 px icon buttons, centred: wishlist (heart, filled and green when present in the wishlist) and quick view (eye, opens the preview modal). Visible at all breakpoints; the reference platform hides these below md, which removes the only way to reach quick view on a phone.
Add to cartA pill button that slides up from the card's lower edge on hover, on desktop only. Label "ADD TO CART". Below md, and on any coarse pointer, it is a permanently visible full-width button in the card footer — hover-only affordances do not exist on touch.
Out of stockAdd to cart replaced by a disabled "OUT OF STOCK" and a secondary "Notify me" that captures an email against the variant.
Multi-variantAdd to cart reads "CHOOSE OPTIONS" and navigates to the product page rather than adding, because there is nothing unambiguous to add.
KeyboardCard is one tab stop; the two icon buttons and add-to-cart are separate stops in reading order. Focus ring is the 2 px primary-green outline. Hover-revealed controls become visible on focus-within.

7.2.4 Quick-view modal

Max width 900 px. Left (5 of 12): the lg image with a thumbnail strip. Right (7 of 12): name, price, stock, a two-line clamped description, variant selector if applicable, quantity stepper, "ADD TO CART", and "View full details" linking to the product page. Close by button, scrim click or Esc. Focus trapped inside while open; returned to the triggering card on close. Adding to cart from the modal closes it and opens the cart off-canvas.

7.4 Product detail

Purpose. Answer "is this the right part, is it in stock, what does it cost" in the first screenful, and support the decision below the fold.

7.4.1 Layout — above the fold

RegionSpecification
Gallery (left, 6 of 12)Main image in a 1:1 well on --ts-surface-sunken, object-fit: contain, using the lg derivative. Click opens a lightbox with pan and zoom; on touch, pinch to zoom. Beneath it a thumbnail strip of sm derivatives, 4 visible with chevron paging, the active one outlined in primary green. Keyboard: ←/→ change image; thumbnails are a tab-navigable radio group.
Buy box (right, 6 of 12)An elevated white card, 1 px border, radius md, shadow 1. Contents in order: product name as h1 (uppercase, 22 px, allowed to wrap to three lines); SKU in monospace muted with a click-to-copy control; average rating stars plus "(n reviews)" linking to the reviews tab, omitted when there are none; price block; variant selector; quantity stepper and stock display side by side; then three full-width stacked buttons.
Price blockCurrent price at 24 px semibold primary green. When discounted: original struck through in muted grey above, then the discounted price, then an amber "SAVE 15%" badge and, if the discount window ends within 7 days, "Offer ends 3 March".
Variant selectorOmitted for single-variant products. One control per attribute, labelled with the attribute name in uppercase. ≤ 6 options render as a pill group; more render as a select. Options that are out of stock are shown struck through and disabled — never hidden, because their absence is information the buyer needs. Choosing a variant updates price, SKU, stock and the gallery if that variant has its own image, and writes the selection to the URL as ?v=SKU so a specific variant is shareable.
QuantityLabel "QUANTITY:". A stepper: − button, numeric input, + button, matching the reference platform's arrangement. Minimum 1; maximum is available stock; the input accepts typing and clamps on blur. − is disabled at 1; + disabled at stock, with a tooltip "Only n available".
StockLabel "AVAILABLE STOCK:" and a read-only field showing the number, as the reference platform does. Above 5: green dot and the count. 1–5: amber dot and "Only n left". 0: red dot and "Out of stock".
ActionsADD TO CART (primary, full width) · ADD TO WISHLIST (secondary) · BUY NOW (primary, adds to cart and navigates straight to checkout). Out of stock: the first and third are replaced by a disabled "OUT OF STOCK" plus "NOTIFY ME WHEN AVAILABLE".

7.4.2 Layout — below the fold

  • Tabs. "DESCRIPTION" and "REVIEWS (n)", as on the reference platform, plus "SPECIFICATIONS" when the product has attributes. Implemented as real ARIA tabs with arrow-key navigation. The panel is not lazily unmounted, so browser find-in-page works across it.
  • Description panel. Sanitised rich text from the admin editor. Permitted elements: p, strong, em, u, ul, ol, li, h3, h4, a, br, table, thead, tbody, tr, th, td, img, code, pre, blockquote. Everything else is stripped server-side on save and client-side on render — sanitising in one place only is how stored XSS happens.
  • Specifications panel. A two-column table of attribute names and values for the selected variant.
  • Reviews panel. Summary at the top: average out of 5, total count, and a five-row histogram with proportional bars. Then a list of reviews, each with rating stars, author display name, relative date, body, and a "Verified purchase" badge where the reviewer has a delivered order containing the product. Sort by most recent or highest rating; paginated at 10. A signed-in user who has not yet reviewed sees the review form; a guest sees "Please sign in to write a review" with a button that opens the auth modal. Own reviews can be edited or deleted.
  • Review form. Required 1–5 star radio group, optional 80-character title, required body of 10–2,000 characters with a live counter. Submits as pending for moderation with the message "Thank you — your review will appear once approved."
  • "RELATED PRODUCTS". Heading, one line of copy, then up to 12 cards in a carousel drawn from the same subcategory, excluding the current product, ordered by sales. Section omitted when empty rather than rendered with a stray heading.
  • Recently viewed. Up to 8 cards from a client-side ring buffer, deduplicated by product id. Omitted when fewer than two entries exist.

7.4.3 States and edge cases

CaseBehaviour
LoadingGallery and buy box render as skeletons at final dimensions. Tabs render with their labels and a skeleton body.
Slug not foundCheck product_slug_history; if the slug was retired, 301 to the current one. Otherwise render the 404 page with a search field and "Browse all products".
Product soft-deleted410 Gone semantics: "This product is no longer available" plus related products from the same subcategory. Not a bare 404 — the page existed and may be linked from elsewhere.
No imagesPlaceholder graphic bearing the TechStar mark. Never a broken image icon.
Every variant out of stockBuy box shows the out-of-stock treatment; the variant selector remains interactive so the buyer can still read prices and register interest.
Price changed since a cached viewThe buy box is authoritative. If the cart holds this item at a different price, the cart shows a change notice on its next revalidation (§12.5).
Quantity exceeds stock at submitServer rejects; the field is clamped to available stock and a toast explains "Only n available — quantity adjusted."

7.4.4 Structured data

Product           name, description (plain text), sku, image[], brand,
                  category, aggregateRating (when reviews exist)
Offer             price, priceCurrency "TZS", availability
                  (InStock | OutOfStock), url, priceValidUntil when discounted
BreadcrumbList    Home > Category > Subcategory > Product
Review[]          author, reviewRating, datePublished, reviewBody

Emitted as a single application/ld+json block. Every value must match what is rendered — divergence between structured data and visible content is a manual-action risk with search engines, not a clever optimisation.

7.5 Search results

Purpose. Serve the electronics buyer who arrived with a part number. Speed and precision matter more than presentation.

  • Heading: "Search results for 'term'" with the count beneath: "28 products found".
  • The same control bar, filter panel, grid and pagination as the catalogue — one component, one behaviour to learn.
  • The searched term is highlighted within result names using a <mark> styled with an amber tint.
  • Matching is specified in §16.2: SKU exact match ranks first, then name prefix, then name contains, then description full-text. A search for "74HC14" must surface that exact part before anything merely mentioning it.
  • Empty state. "No products match 'term'", then in order: spelling suggestion ("Did you mean arduino?") when one is available within an edit distance of 2 of an indexed term; up to 6 popular products; "Browse all categories"; and "Ask us on WhatsApp — we may be able to source it", which turns a dead end into a lead.
  • Every search with zero results is recorded with its term and result count, and surfaced in the admin console (§16.4) as the catalogue's own to-do list.

7.6.2 Cart page

Same data, laid out as a two-column page: a line-item table on the left (8 of 12) with columns Product, Unit price, Quantity, Total, Remove; and an order summary card on the right (4 of 12), sticky on scroll, holding subtotal, estimated shipping once a method is chosen, total, and "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT". Below the table: "Continue shopping" and "Clear cart" (with confirmation). Stacks to one column below lg.

7.6.3 Wishlist off-canvas

Header "WISHLIST". Rows carry thumbnail, name, price, current stock status, "Move to cart" and remove. No quantity — a wishlist entry is an intent, not a line item, which is why the reference platform's unused quantity field is dropped. Empty state: heart icon, "Your wishlist is empty", "Browse products". Signed-in wishlists persist server-side and merge on sign-in per §12.5.4.
