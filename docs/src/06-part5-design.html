Part FiveBrand and Design System

The reference platform's visual system is deliberately plain: a dark utility bar, a dark header with a wide search field, a white category strip, generous white space, flat white product cards on a white page, and a single saturated green used for every affordance. It is not fashionable and it is exactly right for the job — a components catalogue is read, not admired, and the restraint keeps 617 product photographs from fighting each other.

TechStar keeps that system's structure and proportion precisely, and replaces only the identity: name, wordmark and hue. Everything specified below is normative. Where a value is inherited from the reference platform because it works, that is stated.

5.1 Brand definition

AttributeDefinition
NameTechStar Store. Written as two words, both capitalised, no hyphen, no camel case. "TechStar" alone is acceptable in body copy after first use.
WordmarkTECHSTAR in Montserrat ExtraBold, letter-spacing 0.02em, followed by Store in Montserrat Light at the same optical size, closed by a full stop in the primary green. Mirrors the reference lockup's weight contrast without copying its forms.
MarkA four-point star formed from two overlapping chevrons, in primary green on white or white on primary green. Square-safe at 32 px for the favicon; readable at 16 px.
TaglineBuild. Learn. Innovate. — three words, full stops, set in the wordmark's light weight beneath it at roughly 28% of its cap height, in the accent amber.
VoiceDirect and technical. Say what a thing does and what it costs. No exclamation marks in interface copy. Error messages state the problem and the fix, never blame the user.
Clear spaceMinimum clear space around the lockup equals the cap height of T on all sides.
Minimum sizeFull lockup no smaller than 120 px wide. Below that, the mark alone.

5.3 Typography

Montserrat is the single family, inherited from the reference platform and kept: it is geometric, has a wide weight range, renders legibly at small sizes on Android, and is free. Self-host WOFF2 subsets rather than loading from a CDN — it removes a third-party request from the critical path and keeps the page working when the CDN is slow, which on Tanzanian mobile networks is often.
