Part ThirteenPayments in Tanzania

Payment is where an e-commerce specification written for one market fails in another. Card penetration is low, mobile money is near-universal, and the dominant flow is a customer pushing funds to a merchant till number from a USSD menu — an action the website cannot observe. Everything in this part follows from that fact.

13.1 Landscape

ChannelCharacteristics and implications for TechStar
Mobile money — customer-initiated push (Lipa Namba / merchant till)The customer dials a USSD string or uses their wallet app, selects the merchant-payment option, enters the till number and the amount, and confirms with a PIN. The merchant receives an SMS or email notification. No API is involved. This is what the reference platform implements, it works without any commercial integration, and it is what TechStar launches with. The cost is manual reconciliation (§12.8).
Mobile money — merchant-initiated pull (USSD push / STK)The merchant's system asks the network to prompt the customer's handset; the customer approves with a PIN and the merchant is notified over a webhook within seconds. This requires an aggregator or a direct operator integration, and it removes reconciliation entirely. It is the target state, specified as Phase 4.
Cash on deliveryWidely expected, especially for first-time buyers, and materially raises conversion. The cost is delivery risk and a cash-handling process. The reference platform charges a lower shipping fee for it, which is a sensible way to price the risk down.
Bank transferRelevant for institutional buyers — schools, universities, companies — who pay against an invoice. Handled by the invoice module plus manual reconciliation; no integration needed.
CardsLow volume domestically, useful for diaspora and corporate purchases. Available through the same aggregators. Out of scope for v1 (§3.5).

Verify before you publish USSD strings, menu positions and till-number formats change, and they differ between consumer and merchant products. Every instruction block must be verified against the live network — by an actual test payment — before launch, and re-verified whenever an operator changes its menus. That is precisely why the instruction content is stored as editable settings (§14.16) rather than compiled into the front end: correcting a wrong menu step must take two minutes, not a release.

Design pointRationale
Capture the paying numberThe single strongest reconciliation key. Required, validated as 12 digits beginning 255. It is what turns matching from a search into a lookup.
Order number as referenceShown prominently and copyable. Some wallets allow a reference; where they do, it makes matching exact.
Exact amount"Pay exactly TZS 85,000." Round-number habits produce under- and over-payments, and each one costs a phone call.
Reservation window48 hours, communicated at placement and again by SMS at 36 hours. Long enough for someone who pays on payday; short enough that stock is not held indefinitely.
Live statusThe confirmation page subscribes to the order's realtime channel, so verification updates the page the customer is already looking at. It is a small thing that removes a large share of "have you received my payment?" contacts.
No payment proof uploadDeliberately omitted. Screenshots are trivially forged and create a queue of items requiring judgement. The merchant's own notification is the source of truth.

13.3 Aggregator integration

Automation is introduced behind one interface, so that the choice of provider is a configuration decision and never a rewrite. Nothing outside the adapter knows which provider is in use.

interface PaymentProvider {
  readonly key: string                       // 'selcom' | 'clickpesa' | …
  readonly capabilities: {
    mobileMoneyPush: boolean                 // prompt the customer's handset
    hostedCheckout: boolean                  // redirect to a provider page
    cards: boolean
    refunds: boolean
    statusQuery: boolean
  }

  createPayment(input: {
    orderNumber: string
    amount: Money
    customer: { name: string; phone: string; email?: string }
    method: 'mobile_money' | 'card'
    network?: 'mpesa' | 'mixx' | 'airtel' | 'halopesa'
    returnUrl: string
    webhookUrl: string
    idempotencyKey: string
  }): Promise<{
    providerReference: string
    status: 'pending' | 'succeeded' | 'failed'
    redirectUrl?: string                     // hosted checkout
    instructions?: string                    // e.g. "approve the prompt on your phone"
  }>

  verifyWebhook(headers, rawBody): { valid: boolean; event: ProviderEvent }
  queryStatus(providerReference): Promise<PaymentStatus>
  refund(providerReference, amount: Money, reason: string): Promise<RefundResult>
}
CandidateWhat to evaluate at selection time
SelcomEstablished Tanzanian aggregator with broad wallet coverage and a merchant network. Evaluate settlement period, per-transaction pricing and webhook reliability.
ClickPesaDeveloper-oriented, positioned at exactly this use case. Evaluate onboarding time and sandbox quality.
AzamPayLocal aggregator with multi-wallet collection. Evaluate documentation depth and support responsiveness.
DPO Group / PesapalPan-African, card plus mobile money, hosted checkout available. Evaluate pricing on low-value transactions — a TZS 200 resistor order must not cost more in fees than it earns.
Direct operatorVodacom and Airtel offer merchant APIs directly. Better unit economics at volume; heavier onboarding and one integration per network.
Selection criteria, in priority order (1) Coverage of all four major wallets through one integration. (2) Reliable, signed webhooks with documented retry semantics. (3) A sandbox that behaves like production. (4) Settlement within two business days. (5) Per-transaction cost that is viable on a TZS 5,000 basket. (6) Refund support through the API. Providers that fail (2) create more manual work than they remove.

13.4 Cash on delivery

RuleSpecification
Order statePlaced directly as CONFIRMED — payment is not pending in any meaningful sense. Payment status stays unpaid until the courier settles.
StockReserved at placement, deducted at SHIPPED, not at confirmation.
Reservation expiryDoes not apply. The order is confirmed, so there is nothing to expire.
Basket capConfigurable maximum order value for cash on delivery, default TZS 300,000. Above it the method is hidden with an explanation. Unpaid delivery risk scales with basket size and should be bounded.
Verification callStaff telephone the customer to confirm before dispatch. Recorded as an order event, so a repeat no-answer is visible.
SettlementThe courier records collection; staff mark the payment received with the collecting person named. This creates the payments row with method cash.
Refusal on deliveryOrder moves to CANCELLED with reason "refused on delivery"; stock is restored on return to the store. Repeat refusals from one phone number are surfaced to staff on that customer's record.

13.5 Webhooks and idempotency

An aggregator's webhook is the only moment where money and system state meet automatically. It must be impossible to double-credit, and impossible to lose an event.

POST /api/v1/webhooks/payments/:provider

 1  Read the RAW body — never the parsed one. Signature verification over
    re-serialised JSON fails intermittently and inexplicably.
 2  Verify the signature with the provider's secret, constant-time compare.
    Fail → log, return 401, do not process.
 3  Check the source IP against the provider's published range when one exists.
 4  INSERT the event into payment_events, keyed by external_event_id.
    Duplicate key → this is a retry. Return 200 immediately. Do nothing else.
 5  Return 200 NOW. Everything below runs on a queue.
    A provider that does not get a prompt 200 will retry, sometimes forever.
 6  Worker:
      ├─ resolve the order by merchant_reference
      ├─ compare the amount to the expected total
      ├─ update the payments row inside a transaction
      ├─ run the order state transition through the state machine
      ├─ deduct stock with a ledger row
      ├─ write order_events with actor_type = 'webhook'
      ├─ queue customer notifications
      └─ mark payment_events.processed_at
 7  Processing failure → record processing_error, alert staff, leave the event
    replayable from the admin console. The event is never lost.
GuaranteeMechanism
At-most-once creditingUnique index on payment_events.external_event_id, and on payments(provider, provider_reference). Two independent constraints, because this is the one thing that must not fail.
No lost eventsPersist before processing. Every webhook body is stored whether or not it can be handled.
Out-of-order deliveryEvents carry a provider timestamp; an event older than the payment's current state is recorded and ignored.
Unknown orderRecorded with processing_error = 'order_not_found' and raised to staff. Never silently dropped — an unmatched payment is money that arrived.
Amount mismatchNever auto-confirms. Flagged for manual review with both figures shown.
ReplayStaff with payment.verify can replay a stored event from the admin console after a bug fix, without asking the provider to resend.
Reconciliation sweepA scheduled job queries provider status for every payment stuck in pending beyond 30 minutes, catching webhooks that never arrived.

13.6 The reconciliation ledger

Three questions must be answerable at any moment, from the database alone, without a spreadsheet:

  1. What have we been paid? Sum of payments in status succeeded over a period, grouped by method and provider.
  2. What are we owed? Sum of orders.total_amount − paid_amount where the order is not cancelled, expired or refunded.
  3. What arrived that we cannot place? payment_events with a processing error, and payments whose amount does not match their order.
ReportDefinition
Daily settlementEvery payment verified in the day, with order number, method, provider reference, amount and verifier. Exportable to CSV; reconciled against the mobile-money statement.
OutstandingOrders awaiting payment, aged into 0–2 h, 2–24 h and over 24 h buckets. Drives the dashboard alert.
ExceptionsAmount mismatches, unmatched events, duplicate reference attempts, overpayments awaiting refund. Should normally be empty; a persistently non-empty exceptions list is the signal to move to an aggregator.
Refund registerAll refunds with reason, amount, destination number and authorising staff member.
Method mixShare of orders and of revenue by payment method over time — the evidence base for deciding whether cash on delivery is worth its risk and whether automation is worth its fee.
Financial invariant For every order: paid_amount = SUM(payments.amount WHERE status = 'succeeded') − SUM(refunds). A nightly job asserts this across all orders and alerts on any row that fails. An invariant that is checked is a control; one that is merely intended is a hope.
